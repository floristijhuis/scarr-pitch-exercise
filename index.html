How the exercise works

Prepare a brief pitch about your research for a specific type of audience.

In groups of four, everyone spins the wheel for a secret audience: children, friends in different fields, a grant interview or a lab meeting with peers. Keep yours to yourself.

Prepare. You have 15 minutes to prepare a 90-second elevator pitch about your own Bachelor’s thesis, written for your secret audience.

Pitch. Take turns, 5 minutes each. Give your pitch. The others guess the audience and give feedback on the tone and clarity of the pitch.

We will walk around. Good luck!
